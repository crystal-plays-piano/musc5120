# Concert Report

For this Concert Report, I attended the "Tapestry of Sounds" Concert as performed
by the WashU Symphony Orchestra. I attended the performance on the evening of the 16th
of October.

This concert was presented as a sort of "overview" concert, with music spanning hundreds of years
and many styles - in this element, I am a little disappointed with the results. The concert programme
is as follows:

1. Overture to *Nabucco* by Giuseppe Verdi

2. Piano Concerto in A minor Op. 16 by Edvard Grieg

3. *A Shelter that Filters the Sun* by Armando Bayolo

4. *Soirées Musicales*, Op. 9 by Benjamin Britten.
